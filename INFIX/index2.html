Prefix Evaluation

Evaluate a **prefix** expression (e.g., `+ 2 3`) to find its numerical result. The evaluation process is often implemented using a stack by scanning the expression from right to left.

Postfix Evaluation

Evaluate a **postfix** expression (e.g., `2 3 +`) to compute its value. This is the most efficient method for expression evaluation and is widely used in calculators and virtual machines.
